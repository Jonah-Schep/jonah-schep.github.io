01

CMDA Capstone
Spring 2026

Industry partner: Ozmo

My contribution Nearly all of the project

Giving every support agent the best agent's playbook

Telecom support transcripts show what successful agents do, but only as unstructured conversation. I built a Databricks framework that turns those transcripts into live suggestions, with the agent making the final call.

  • Built a nine-stage pipeline on Databricks and Delta Lake that masks eight PII types with ai_mask() before any LLM call.
  • Clustered 9,604 agent utterances into 18 reusable actions with TF-IDF and KMeans, choosing k by silhouette and elbow, and labeled only the clusters with an LLM to control cost.
  • Served playbooks through Mosaic Vector Search. Agents see three suggestions, and overrides are logged as training signal.
  • Tested BERTopic for category discovery and dropped it after 7 of 15 categories scored F1 = 0.

03

Data Science in Business
Fall 2025

Fictional company: CyberGuard

My contribution About 50%, with Rowan Muhoberac

Detecting rare network attacks and pricing the deployment

Every attack class sat below 0.4% prevalence, which makes accuracy meaningless. We built four specialized classifiers, scored them on precision and recall, and turned the detection gains into a business case.

  • Trained XGBoost classifiers for advanced persistent threats, phishing, zero-day exploits, and insider threats across 525,000 connections.
  • Caught every APT, phishing, and zero-day case in evaluation. Insider threats were the hardest class, at 75.61% detection and 0.83 PR-AUC.
  • Built a simulator and cost-benefit model for a $1.2M project, with break-even in about one year.
